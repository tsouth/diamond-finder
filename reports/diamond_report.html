How the rankings are calculated

1. Qualification gate

D/E color, VS1 or better, Excellent polish and symmetry, and IGI Hexagonal Modified Brilliant are required. Step cuts are rejected.

2. Dimensional fit

Target: 15.5 × 7.8 mm. Penalty = ((length − 15.5) / 0.35)² + ((width − 7.8) / 0.20)². Width is intentionally weighted more heavily.

3. Soft preferences

Ratio 1.90–2.05, table 55–65%, and depth 58–67% have no penalty. Small deviations lower the score but do not automatically reject a stone.

4. Evidence

Unverified cutting style and missing optical review lower confidence. IGI data and stone-specific imagery are tracked separately from retailer labels.

5. Diamond score

100 − 5 × (dimension + proportion + evidence penalties). Higher is better. Price is excluded so the best criteria match remains visible.

6. Value and price

The value score adds cheapest effective price ÷ 4,000 as a modest penalty. Retailer prices are also compared directly; promotions remain conditional until checkout.

Important: Neither score predicts brilliance, bow tie, scintillation, contrast, windowing, or real-world appearance. Those require structured optical review.
