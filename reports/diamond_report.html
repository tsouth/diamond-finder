How we picked the best matches

Start here if you want to understand the recommendations. The detailed math is available, but you do not need it to compare the stones.

First, the essentials

Only D/E color, VS1 or better, Excellent polish and symmetry, and the correct IGI cutting style make the list.

Then, the right size

Stones closest to your 15.5 × 7.8 mm target rank higher. Width matters a little more because it has a strong effect on the face-up look.

Your preferred shape

A 1.90–2.05 ratio, 55–65% table, and 58–67% depth are preferred. A small miss lowers the match score; it does not automatically rule a stone out.

Confidence matters

A verified IGI report and stone-specific photos or video make a recommendation more trustworthy. Retailer descriptions alone are not enough.

Diamond score

This is the overall match for your size, proportions, and available evidence. Higher is better, and price is deliberately left out.

Value score

This adds price to the picture, helping you spot a strong match at a competitive price. Any promotion should still be confirmed at checkout.

One important note: Neither score predicts brilliance or how the diamond will look in person. Photos, video, and a careful visual review are still essential.
